Questions

Is this another lead database?

No. Quest looks for people describing the problem you solve, now — not for static profiles.

Will it post for me?

No. Quest finds the person and drafts a reply; you read it, change what you like, and post it yourself.

Do I need a SaaS product?

No. A product, a service, a portfolio — if people ask for what you do, Quest can look for them.

Where does it look?

Where your buyers already talk about the problem — Reddit communities first. Quest picks them from what you sell.

Why not just use ChatGPT?

Because Quest keeps looking when you are not talking to it, and comes back when it finds someone.
