# SEO & GEO — what we know, and where to scale

Everything measured or sourced on **2026-08-30**. Dates matter in this file: the
mechanism it describes is three weeks old and one of its numbers moved 86% in four days.

---

## 1. The mechanism changed on 2026-08-08

ChatGPT added **domain-scoped `site:` queries**: 0.37% → **16.8% of all fanout
queries in one day** (~46×), and searches per response went 1.08 → 1.83, additive.
It now decides *which domain to ask*, then scopes to it. Discussion platforms are
almost never the target of a `site:` query about a product; **official sites,
documentation and institutional domains are.**

Six days later the citation data caught up. Where ChatGPT's slots went:

| Category | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Help centres + product docs | ~2% | **32%** |
| Established company sites | 4% | 18% |
| App marketplaces | 2% | **17%** |
| Small company sites + **blogs** | 66% | **32%** |

**Consequence:** a blog-shaped site is in the only category that halved. A
documentation-shaped site is in the one that grew 16×. Same pages, opposite outcome.

## 2. Engine mix (Aug 2026) — they have diverged

| Engine | Reddit | YouTube | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Perplexity | 13.9% | **31.2%** | Wikipedia 7.2% |
| Google AI Overviews | 18.5% | 21.1% | Facebook 10.7% |
| Google AI Mode | 19.9% | 17.0% | google.com 11.5%, self-citation rising |
| ChatGPT Search | **0.52%** (was 3.83%) | 1.8% | Wikipedia dominant |
| Claude | **0.01%** | 0.02% | effectively never |

Only **2.7% of domains overlap across all five engines**. Google's mix stayed
comparatively stable through the August shift.

**Caveat, load-bearing:** level estimates disagree by ~30× depending on
methodology (Obsurfable puts ChatGPT-Reddit at 16.7% the same month Promptwatch
puts ChatGPT-Search-Reddit at 0.52% — different denominators). Direction and
volatility are reliable; levels are not. Reddit's ChatGPT share has collapsed and
recovered before. **Build nothing that only pays off if August holds.**

---

## 3. Why scale is the right bet

- Evertune, 200M prompts / 5 months: **no domain reliably exceeds 5%** of citations.
  Wikipedia + Reddit + LinkedIn + YouTube *combined* rarely top 5%. **The other 95%
  spreads across thousands of domains.**
- Top 10 sites = **12%** of AI citations, against ~66% of clicks for the top 10 in
  classic search.

AI citation is a **long tail, not winner-take-all**. Coverage is the correct
strategy; you cannot outrank Wikipedia and do not need to.

## 4. Platform priority (text content, ChatGPT-first)

Reddit is excluded here on purpose — it stays the **product** channel, not an SEO one.

| # | Surface | Evidence | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Own domain, docs-shaped** | the 2%→32% category; the `site:` fanout target | the main build |
| 2 | **App marketplaces** — Chrome Web Store, MCP directories | 2%→17%, "most listings are half-filled" | **days** |
| 3 | **Wikidata + Knowledge Graph** | ChatGPT grounds entities there; a precondition for being `site:`-scoped at all | **hours** |
| 4 | **LinkedIn long-form articles** | 14.3% of ChatGPT responses; #11→#5; #1 for professional queries. **Articles 500–2,000w = 50–66% of cited LinkedIn content; 59% of citations from individual creators, not company pages** | weekly |
| 5 | **Quora** | 4.1× ChatGPT citation multiplier, highest of any community platform | weekly |
| 6 | **G2 / Capterra** | corroboration; one vendor flow covers both since Feb 2026 | low |
| — | YouTube | #1 on Perplexity and AI Overviews, but **1.8% on ChatGPT** and not text | deferred |

**85% of brand mentions originate on third-party pages.** This cannot be an
owned-domain-only strategy however good the docs are.

## 5. Retrieval and structure

- ChatGPT Search retrieves through **Bing's index** via OAI-SearchBot. **A page not
  in Bing cannot be cited**, regardless of structure. IndexNow is therefore priority one.
- Retrieval ≠ citation: ChatGPT cites **~15%** of pages it retrieves.
- FAQ schema + inline citations ≈ **40% higher** selection weight.
- Structured pages get ≈ **3× more citations** than equivalent prose.
- Original research and unique benchmarks are citation magnets.
- Only **12%** of ChatGPT-cited URLs rank in Google's top 10; **44%** of SaaS brands
  with strong Google rankings have zero ChatGPT visibility. **SEO and GEO are two
  scoreboards — track both, never let one stand in for the other.**

**The compose rule:** one clear claim per section, as a full sentence, with the
product name and the specific number in the same sentence. Engines quote sentences,
not pages.

## 7. Where to scale

Target **100k pages, documentation-shaped**, in ten families. Ranked topic list and
its generator live outside this repo (`topics.csv`, 10,000 rows, 29 columns).

Order of operations that actually matters:

1. **Identity before volume** — Wikidata item + marketplace listings filled. Days.
2. **Bing/IndexNow before publishing** — it is the retrieval gate.
3. **Docs family first** despite being ~0.4% of URLs — it is what a `site:` fanout lands on.
4. **Q&A family largest** — question + direct answer is the most-cited structure.
5. **Phase submission** 10k → 40k → 100k via sitemap shards. A two-day-old domain
   declaring 100k URLs at once is a spam signal.
6. **Gate hard**: class-A measurement ≥60% of word count, no verbatim platform
   content in body text, one claim per sentence, inline citation per number. A
   refused draft is a 404, never a thin page.

Expect by week 4: **8k–25k indexed**, not 100k. Head-term #1 is a 6–12 month
outcome; four weeks buys the asset plus long-tail positions.

## 8. Open decisions

1. **Community-page facts.** Keyless Reddit reads are blocked. Options: ship without
   the 44k community pages (stop at ~56k), read via the extension fleet's own
   sessions, or the commercial contract. Recommended: ship without, pursue the
   contract, treat the fleet option as its own explicit opt-in — users consented to
   their browser reading Reddit *for their own outreach*, which is a different purpose.
2. **Subreddit list is unvalidated** — names in the topic CSV come from working
   knowledge, not a live pull. Validate before any `/r/` page publishes.
3. **Competitor facts unverified** — do not publish a pricing claim not yet checked.
