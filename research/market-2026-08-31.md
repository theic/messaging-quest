# Market check — is the gap still there? (2026-08-31)

Web research by a background agent on 2026-08-31; every claim carries its
source and date, UNVERIFIED means exactly that. Kept in the repo for the same
reason every measurement here is dated: this is the kind of picture that is
wrong later in a way nobody notices.

## Verdict, in one paragraph

**Narrowing — and which gap matters has changed.** The "GEO + community
listening + reply drafting" combo is no longer unshipped: ReddGrow (launched
Feb 2026, $9,733 MRR as of today), RedReplier and Vismore all ship it hosted,
and Adobe/Semrush(+Brand24) and HubSpot AEO ($50/mo) are converging from
above. "Etiquette-first" as a *message* is commoditized — half the category
advertises "never auto-posts". What remains genuinely open is the exact
intersection earshot occupies: **open-source + local-first + user-owned
memory, with enforced (not advertised) refusals, exposed via MCP/agent
skills.** The whole OSS field is three repos under 50 stars, none of which
draft, judge against user rules, or check GEO; the OpenClaw marketing
ecosystem (200k-star channel) holds only markdown skill packs and one *paid
hosted* service squatting the Reddit-listening slot.

## Open-source / local competition

- OpenMagpie — 43★, self-hosted listening + local-LLM scoring. No drafting, no GEO.
- harken — 17★. Postiz — 35.3k★ but publishing, not listening.

- **No open standard exists. But revealed demand for OSS listening is tiny**
  (≤43★ everywhere; a GummySearch-alternative ask on HN: 1 point, 0 comments).

## GEO tier

Profound (~$1B valuation, $99/mo entry), Peec ($10M ARR, 3k customers),
Otterly/Scrunch/Goodie ($29–495/mo), Semrush AI Toolkit $99/mo (Adobe
acquiring Semrush; Brand24 folding in), Ahrefs Brand Radar realistic
$828+/mo, HubSpot AEO $50/mo standalone (Apr 2026). **None of the leaders
does subreddit-level listening or drafting** — citation analytics only. The
combo exists only at the indie tier.

## The buyer

- Founders pay $10–50/mo; $99+ survives only under GEO framing; loud
  subscription resistance (IH post selling $49 one-time, 220+ comments).
- Ban fear is the #1 objection to the whole category.
- GummySearch refugees (135k) are a named, courtable cohort.
- Demand signal is stronger for **agent-native** (MCP as a paid feature on
  $119–159/mo plans) than for **open-source** per se.

## Platform risk — the red flag

Chronology of Reddit's read-side lockdown:
- 2025-06: sues Anthropic. 2025-10: sues Perplexity + SerpApi (claims
  survived dismissal 2026-07-31).
- 2025-11: "Responsible Builder Policy" — new API keys gated behind manual
  approval; GummySearch dies despite seeking a license.
- 2026-05-28: unauthenticated `.json` deprecated (403 by ~May 30); **RSS
  explicitly named "a common surface for scraping"; mods surveyed about it.**
- **2026-06-11: RSS throttled to ~1 request/minute per IP** — which means our
  measured `ANON_GAP_MS = 65s` (measured 2026-08-28) was a measurement of
  *this throttle*, and we sit exactly at the ceiling with zero headroom.
- Precedent: X's C&D killed Nitter and XCancel (2026-08-26). Logged-out
  reader lanes get closed regardless of good behaviour.
- Known survivable fallbacks: Reddit's **private feed token** (account-bound
  personal RSS, works post-throttle — unmeasured by us), and logged-in
  reading through the user's own browser (our planned browser lane).
- Shadow-removal checking is commoditized: Reveddit is free and maintained.
- LinkedIn: Q1 2026 crackdown restricted ~40% of accounts on the named
  automation tools within 48h; read-only and manual-send models were not
  named.

## What this changes (decision inputs, not yet decisions)

1. **Browser lane + hub are promoted from roadmap to survival plan.** The
   anonymous-RSS pillar has zero headroom and a dated eviction warning; the
   architectures that survive are "the user's own logged-in browser, human-
   paced" and "one machine reads, many pull".
2. **Measure the private feed token** as a compliant fallback for finding
   (account-bound; the *stranger check* stays logged-out at low volume).
3. **sync/check is the hook, not the moat** (Reveddit exists). The moat is
   memory + enforced refusals + the open engine behind agent surfaces.
4. **Etiquette must be demonstrable, not stated** — receipts (quoted room
   rules, refusal logs, `es ready`) are the differentiation now that the
   words are commoditized.
5. **The ClawHub Reddit-listening slot is held by a paywalled skill.** An
   actually-open engine + skill aimed at that slot is the cheapest
   distribution land-grab available.
6. **Hosted twin must land in the $19–49/mo band**; GEO-only pricing gets
   squeezed to a $50–99 bundle line item by the suites.

## Addendum, 2026-09-01: okara.ai — the CMO shape, observed live

Observed on the operator's own project (screenshot, okara.ai/agent/cmo/…):
"Hire your AI CMO" at **$129/month**. The anatomy is exactly the shape this
repo is building, which is worth writing down because it confirms the shape
and prices the alternative:

- One CMO over an **Agents Feed** of purpose-specific agents — Reddit ("2
  opportunities ready"), SEO, GEO ("2 citation gaps detected"), X, X
  Influencer, LinkedIn, Articles, UGC Videos — each behind an **Upgrade**
  wall.
- **Documents** (Product Information, Marketing Strategy; Competitor
  Analysis/Design Guide/Content Strategy locked) — their version of the
  memory files, partly paywalled.
- An analytics pane (SEO / Links / Technical / GEO tabs, Core Web Vitals),
  Google service connectors, a chat box, a credits meter.

Read against this repo: the Agents Feed is the deck; the specialized agents
are skills with agent seats; the documents are `.mq/` memory the operator
owns as markdown. The difference being defended is not the shape — it is
that here every one of those agents is an open folder anybody can write,
choose between, or replace, and nothing is ever submitted by the machine.
The $129/month is the number to quote when someone asks what "free and
local-first" is competing against.
